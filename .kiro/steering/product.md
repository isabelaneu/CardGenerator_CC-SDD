# Product Overview

This project creates an interactive business card generator where users can register professional identity data and preview a business card before exporting it as an image.

## Core Capabilities

- Collect professional details such as name, role, company, contact information, and brand styling.
- Generate a polished business card preview from the filled form.
- Export the rendered business card as an image file.
- Keep the implementation simple and dependency-free with HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.

## Target Use Cases

- Personal branding and professional profile creation.
- Creating a quick business card for a freelancer, consultant, or small team.
- Designing a static business card layout inside the browser.

## Value Proposition

The product provides a fast way to create and export a professional-looking business card without introducing a build system or external services.
